Privacy Policy

LifeKindled  ·  Last updated: June 1, 2026

LifeKindled is a Bible reading and community app built for church youth groups. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Who We Are

LifeKindled is developed and maintained by Eternity Works. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at:

eternityworks.dev@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use LifeKindled:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password when you create an account
  • Profile data — your display name, chosen avatar, and role within your group (member or leader)
  • Group data — the group you belong to, your group ID, and your invite code if you are a leader
  • Reading activity — reading plan progress, which days you have marked as complete
  • Annotations and highlights — notes and highlights you create while reading the Bible in the app
  • Discussion posts — comments you post in group discussions and likes you give to other comments
  • Prayer and worship sessions — duration of prayer and worship sessions you log in the app
  • Device information — basic device and operating system information collected automatically by Firebase

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Connect you with your church group and display group activity
  • Track your reading plan progress and habit streaks
  • Save your Bible annotations, highlights, and notes
  • Enable group discussions and community features
  • Show leaders insights about group engagement and activity
  • Improve the app over time

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not sell your data to any third party. Ever.

4. Information Visible to Others

By default, your annotations and notes are private and only visible to you. You can choose to share an annotation with your group — if you do, members of your group will be able to see it.

Your display name, avatar, and reading activity (such as completing a reading day) may be visible to other members of your group as part of the community features of the app.

Group leaders and admins can see member names, email addresses, and engagement data such as reading progress and session times within the Leader Dashboard.

5. Third-Party Services

LifeKindled uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Firebase (Google) — used for user authentication, database storage, and app infrastructure. Firebase may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • API.Bible (American Bible Society) — used to load Bible passages in the app. Requests to this service include the book and chapter you are reading but do not include any personally identifiable information. Their privacy policy is available at scripture.api.bible.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which uses industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. We implement Firestore security rules to ensure users can only access data they are authorized to view.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

7. Children's Privacy

LifeKindled is designed for use by church youth groups and may be used by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us at eternityworks.dev@gmail.com and we will take steps to remove that information.

LifeKindled is intended to be used under the supervision of a church leader or pastor who administers the group.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Leave a group at any time from within the app

To request account deletion or a copy of your data, contact us at eternityworks.dev@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal information from our active databases within 30 days. Some data may remain in backups for a limited period before being permanently deleted.

Annotations and highlights you have shared publicly with your group may remain visible to group members until manually deleted.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users within the app. Continued use of LifeKindled after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
